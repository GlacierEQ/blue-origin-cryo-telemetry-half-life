**Company lens:** Blue Origin

## Impact model

This repository now treats impact as executable architecture rather than a prose afterthought. `src/impact_assurance.py` evaluates every consequential change across seven dimensions: near-term value, long-term leverage, failure blast radius, reversibility, evidence strength, company fit, and cross-repository compounding.

## Near-term impact

The strongest immediate value is making **Cryo Telemetry Half-Life** easier to operate, test, compare, and evolve without losing its domain-specific mechanism. Changes with high useful value but weak evidence or excessive blast radius are automatically pushed toward hardening instead of promotion.

## Far-term impact

The project can now participate in a portfolio-wide optimization loop: the same impact vector is comparable across the 53-company innovation fleet while the underlying mechanism remains specialized for Blue Origin. That creates a common decision language without flattening the implementations into one generic system.

## Failure impact

Failure cost is explicitly modeled. High-blast changes lose priority unless evidence and reversibility materially contain the risk. This makes architectural ambition compatible with recovery and prevents locally impressive changes from degrading the wider estate.

## Expansion frontier

Compound this repository upward by feeding real benchmark/runtime evidence into the impact vector, then use the resulting assessment to choose the next mechanism-level enhancement. The objective is higher domain capability and stronger proof simultaneously—not documentation growth for its own sake.
